Shortcut Alarm

iPhone · iOS 26 or later

Alarms that follow your conditions.

Shortcut Alarm lets you set time and geofence alarms, then decide when they should ring with optional conditions. This site provides support and the app’s privacy policy and terms of use.

  • Time and place — Schedule a time or use a geofence to trigger a reminder.
  • Optional conditions — Restrict reminders by time, location, weather, temperature or UV level.
  • Your alarm library — Configure repeat days, holiday mode, labels and sounds. Settings and imported audio are stored on your device.

Free & Pro

Free features. Optional Pro access.

The free tier supports up to three alarms. Pro unlocks additional alarm capacity and weather, temperature and UV conditions. Available subscriptions, one-time unlocks and prices are shown in the app before purchase.

Support

Common questions

Why did an alarm not ring?

Check whether the alarm is enabled and its repeat days and conditions are satisfied. Confirm alarm and location permissions as applicable. Background checks, forecasts and location events depend on system availability.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

Use Restore Purchases in the app for eligible purchases. Manage subscriptions in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can any song become an alarm sound?

Import requires music permission and an accessible, exportable song. Protected subscription music and unavailable local files may not be supported.
