Privacy Policy

Applies to: Shortcut Alarm · Effective date: 2026-10-26

Your alarm settings and imported audio are stored on your device. Shortcut Alarm has no app account, advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Location, map, weather and purchase features use Apple services as described below.

We do not operate a server that receives your alarm library, location or imported music. Contacting support or visiting this website involves separate data processing.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes the current Shortcut Alarm iPhone app and its support website. “We” means the app developer identified in the App Store listing. You can contact us at 617646201lzy@gmail.com. Apple and the website host process information under their own policies where described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The app uses local storage to provide and maintain the features you select. It does not upload this information to a developer-operated account or synchronization service.

  • Alarms: times, repeat days, labels, enabled states, sounds, colors, snooze options and reminder settings.
  • Conditions and places: time windows, selected places and coordinates, geofence radii, inside/outside rules, weather, temperature and UV conditions.
  • Preferences and operating records: holiday overrides, imported-song names and file identifiers, system-alarm identifiers, and local settings used for scheduling and review prompts.
  • Location and weather cache: the latest saved scheduling coordinate and timestamp, and downloaded hourly forecast data with its location and fetch time.
  • Imported audio: a local copy of the song you choose, converted to an audio file for use as an alarm sound.

3. Location, maps and background use

With your permission, the app accesses your location to show your position on a map, configure geofence alarms, evaluate location conditions and obtain weather for your location. Saved places can reveal sensitive information about your routines.

When relevant alarms or conditions require it, Always location permission allows location-related checks while the app is not in the foreground. The app uses system region monitoring and significant-location-change monitoring where needed; it keeps a recent scheduling coordinate locally rather than a travel-history timeline.

Map display, place searches and address lookup use Apple MapKit. Search text, map areas and coordinates needed for a request are sent to Apple services. See Apple Maps & Privacy and Apple Privacy Policy.

You can change location access and Precise Location in iOS Settings. Denying or limiting access can prevent location-dependent alarms or conditions from working as configured. Revoking permission does not erase coordinates already saved in the app.

4. Weather, temperature and UV information

When a weather-related condition is used, the app requests an hourly forecast from Apple WeatherKit using a location coordinate and forecast time range. It caches the response locally to evaluate conditions and reduce repeat requests. It does not send your alarm labels or imported music with the forecast request.

Apple describes WeatherKit location information as used to provide forecasts without association with personally identifiable information or tracking between requests. See Apple WeatherKit and Apple Privacy Policy for Apple’s handling of this service.

5. Alarm and music permissions

Alarm authorization lets the app create and manage alarms through the iOS alarm system. Necessary alarm settings, including timing, presentation and sound, are provided to that system. Labels may be visible in system alarm interfaces, such as the Lock Screen.

Music-library permission is requested when you choose to import a song. The app uses the selected item’s title, identifier and accessible audio to create a local alarm sound; it does not upload the library or the song to us. Revoking music access does not remove audio already imported. Protected or unavailable audio may not be importable.

Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. Refusing an optional permission affects the corresponding feature rather than creating a separate app account.

6. Purchases and Apple diagnostics

Apple processes payments and manages your Apple Account and payment details. The app uses StoreKit product and verified transaction information, such as product identifiers, entitlement status and revocation status, to unlock Pro and restore eligible purchases. We do not receive your card details through the app or send these transactions to a developer-operated verification server.

Apple may provide developers with App Store reports and diagnostics according to its services and your settings. The app does not embed a separate advertising, behavioral analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Apple’s own processing is described in Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Support and this website

If you email 617646201lzy@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and keep necessary correspondence. Please avoid sending precise locations, private alarm labels or unrelated personal information unless needed to explain your issue. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver the service.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host processes technical request data, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages contain no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, external fonts or developer-set tracking cookies. Opening an external link is subject to the destination’s policies.

8. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell or rent your personal information or share it for cross-app advertising. Apple services, GitHub hosting and email delivery process the information necessary for their respective services. We may disclose information we actually hold if required by law, or as necessary to protect rights and security in accordance with applicable law.

Service providers may process information outside your country under their own safeguards and policies. This is separate from the alarm library stored locally on your device; we do not maintain an overseas copy of that library on our own server.

9. Retention, deletion and security

Alarm records and local settings remain until you change or remove them, or delete the app’s data. Recent-location values and weather cache can remain after a related alarm is deleted and are replaced as the app updates them. Deleting an alarm does not necessarily delete a song imported for reuse.

You can delete alarms in the app and reset manual holiday overrides. To remove the app’s remaining local database, imported audio and preferences, use Delete App in iOS. Offload App retains documents and data. Device or iCloud backups may retain earlier copies according to your Apple backup settings; the app does not offer its own cloud backup or synchronization service.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support records or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by email. Apple retains purchase records under its policies; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

The app relies on iOS storage protections and system services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device and operating system protected and up to date.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You control local data through the app and iOS Settings. We cannot access, retrieve or delete an alarm database stored only on your device remotely.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact 617646201lzy@gmail.com to exercise these rights. We may request proportionate information to verify the request. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, optional device access relies on your permission; handling support requests is necessary to respond to you or based on our legitimate interest in supporting the app; information may also be kept to meet legal obligations. Separate providers determine the basis for their own processing.

11. Children and policy updates

The app is a general-purpose reminder tool and does not ask for an age or create child profiles. If a child sends personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request appropriate action. App Store age ratings and local consent requirements still apply.

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change and revise the effective date. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. Questions about this policy can be sent to 617646201lzy@gmail.com.
