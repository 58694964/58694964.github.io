Privacy Policy

Applies to: Shortcut Alarm for iOS  ·  Effective date: September 9, 2026

The short version. Shortcut Alarm does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours for your data to be sent to. Every alarm you create, every place you save, and every setting you change stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone.

The app asks for three system permissions — alarms, location, and your music library — and each one is used only for the feature it belongs to. The single piece of information that ever leaves your device is a geographic coordinate, sent to Apple's weather service to look up a forecast, and only for alarms that actually use a weather, temperature, or UV condition.

The rest of this document explains that in detail.

1. Scope of Information Collection

1.1 We do not collect personal information. The App has no user accounts, no registration, no sign-in, and no third-party login. It does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, or any other identifier, and it does not generate an advertising identifier or any other persistent user ID.

1.2 The App creates and stores the following information locally on your device, in the App's own sandboxed storage. This information is never transmitted to us:

  • Alarms you create — the time or place that triggers them, the conditions attached to them, the reminder mode, the label, the colour, the snooze setting, and whether the alarm is enabled.
  • Places you save for geofence alarms and location conditions — the coordinate you picked on the map, the radius, and the address text shown for your convenience.
  • Ringtone choices — which built-in tone you selected, or a reference to a song in your music library. The audio file itself is never copied out of your music library.
  • App preferences — such as whether tones are previewed when you switch between them, and any manual edits you make to the public-holiday calendar.
  • A cached weather forecast — kept briefly so the app does not have to fetch the same forecast repeatedly. It is overwritten by the next fetch and removed when the App is deleted.

1.3 The App contains no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It does not record which screens you open, which buttons you tap, or how often you use it.

2. How We Collect Information

2.1 Alarm permission. The App uses the system alarm framework built into iOS so that its alarms behave like the ones in the built-in Clock app — they can sound through silent mode and Focus. Granting this permission lets the App register alarms with the system. It does not give the App access to your existing alarms in other apps, and it does not transmit anything.

2.2 Location permission. Location is read on your device for three purposes, and only when an alarm actually needs it:

  • To watch the boundary of a place you set for a geofence alarm, so that the alarm can fire when you enter or leave it. iOS performs this monitoring; the App is simply told when a crossing occurs.
  • To compare your current position with a place you set as a location condition.
  • To determine which coordinate a weather forecast should be requested for.

iOS only delivers boundary crossings to apps that hold "Always" access, which is why the App asks for it. If you grant "While Using the App" instead, the App keeps working, but geofence alarms and background condition checks become unreliable — the App tells you this on the screens where it matters, and never silently pretends otherwise. You can change or revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

2.3 Music library permission. If you choose to use one of your own songs as an alarm sound, the App needs permission to browse your music library so you can pick a track. Only the reference needed to play that track is stored, locally. The App does not read, copy, upload, or index the rest of your library, and it does not access your library at all if you never use this feature.

2.4 The App does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, reminders, health data, or files.

3. How We Use Information

3.1 All information described in Section 1 is used for one purpose only: to decide whether, and at what moment, one of your alarms should ring. That decision is made on your device.

3.2 The App does not use your information for profiling, personalised advertising, recommendation, marketing, or automated decision-making that produces any legal or similarly significant effect on you.

3.3 The App does not sell your information, and it does not monetise it in any form.

4. Third-Party Sharing, Transfer and Public Disclosure

4.1 We do not share, transfer, or publicly disclose your personal information, because we do not collect any.

4.2 Weather data. The one exception worth stating plainly: when an alarm uses a weather, temperature, or UV condition, the App sends a geographic coordinate to Apple's weather service in order to receive a forecast for that location. This request is made by the operating system on the App's behalf. It carries no identifier of you or of your device that we create, and the response is used only to evaluate that alarm's condition. Apple's handling of these requests is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. If you never use a weather, temperature, or UV condition, no such request is ever made.

4.3 The App Store. Downloading and updating the App, and the rating prompt the App may occasionally show, are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We receive no personal information through them — only anonymous, aggregated sales and usage statistics that Apple provides to all developers and that cannot identify an individual user.

4.4 There is no other third party. The App integrates no advertising network, no analytics provider, no social sign-in, and no cloud backend of ours.

5. How Information Is Stored and Protected

5.1 Where information is stored

All information the App creates is stored in the App's own sandboxed container on your iPhone. iOS isolates this container from other apps and protects it with the device's own encryption, which is tied to your passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. We operate no server, no database, and no backup service, so there is no copy of your information anywhere outside your device — other than a copy inside your own encrypted iPhone or iCloud device backup, which is under your control and Apple's, not ours.

5.2 How long information is kept

Information is kept for as long as you keep the alarm that uses it. Deleting an alarm removes its data. Deleting the App removes everything, permanently and immediately. Because none of it ever reaches us, there is nothing left for us to retain, and no retention period for us to define.

5.3 Security

The App transmits nothing that would need to be secured in transit, apart from the weather request described in Section 4.2, which is made over an encrypted connection by the operating system. Because there is no account and no server, the usual risks — credential leaks, database breaches, session hijacking — do not apply to this App.

6. Protection of Minors

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age. Since no personal information is collected at all, no special processing of children's information takes place. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about the App, please contact us using the address in Section 10.

7. Managing Your Information

7.1 System permission settings

You can review, change, or revoke the alarm, location, and music library permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, or in iOS Settings → Shortcut Alarm. Revoking a permission disables the feature that depends on it; the App will tell you which alarms are affected rather than failing silently.

7.2 Viewing and correcting your information

Everything the App stores is visible in the App itself. Open any alarm to see and edit its trigger, conditions, reminder mode, and sound.

7.3 Deleting your information

Swipe an alarm away to delete it and everything attached to it. To remove all of it at once, delete the App from your device. No request to us is required, and none is possible — we hold nothing to delete.

7.4 Portability and access requests

Rights such as access, portability, and erasure normally require a request to the operator holding your data. Here, all of that data is already in your hands and under your sole control. If you nonetheless wish to confirm what the App stores, contact us using the address in Section 10 and we will explain it in detail.

8.2 If the App were ever to stop being maintained, your information would remain unaffected, because it never left your device.
