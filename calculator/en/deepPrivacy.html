1. Information covered

The app stores the following in its local app storage to provide and restore its features:

We do not automatically upload your expressions, amounts, history, notes, tags or memory values. The app has no account, advertising identifier, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or music. Reading the system region to choose an initial currency does not read your location.

2. How information is obtained

The app reads clipboard text when you explicitly choose Paste. iOS controls any cross-app paste permission. Clipboard text is processed locally and is not sent to the developer or exchange-rate services.

Copy writes the text you choose to the system clipboard. Share and CSV export use the system share interface; a recipient, app or destination you select may receive the exported content and apply its own privacy practices. We do not automatically send exported records to a recipient. Temporary export files are removed after the share flow completes; iOS also manages temporary storage.

3. How information is used

Local information is used to calculate, restore input, manage history and memory, apply preferences and limit system review requests. Exchange-rate requests obtain reference quotes. We do not use this information for advertising or profiling, or sell it.

4. Third-party sharing, transfer and disclosure

The app connects over HTTPS to Frankfurter for fiat quotes, ExchangeRate-API as fallback, and Yadio for optional BTC quotes. It requests reference rate tables; it does not attach your amount, expression, notes, tags, history, clipboard text or an app-generated user identifier.

A network connection necessarily makes connection information such as your IP address and technical request headers available to the receiving service and its infrastructure. Whether such information is logged or retained depends on the provider’s practices. Frankfurter publishes its privacy information on its documentation page; ExchangeRate-API’s terms include a privacy policy. Yadio publishes its service terms.

Quotes and refresh metadata are saved locally. Failed updates keep existing cached quotes. Third-party infrastructure may process requests outside your country. We do not operate the rate providers or control their retention policies.

Downloads, updates, system ratings and any diagnostics you choose to share through iOS are handled by Apple under Apple’s settings and policies. The app may request Apple’s native rating interface after use. The counter and cooldown for this request stay local; the app cannot determine your star rating, whether you submitted a review, or whether the prompt appeared. Public App Store reviews may be visible to the developer and other people.

These static pages are hosted by GitHub through GitHub Pages. The pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies or a feedback-submission backend. GitHub may process technical connection information under its own privacy statement. Clicking an external link or sending email is handled by the destination service.

5. Storage and protection

Local records remain until you remove them, replace the relevant data, or use Delete App in iOS. Offload App normally retains local data. We use the app sandbox and iOS data protection for local storage, and HTTPS for rate requests; no system can promise absolute security.

Your device backup settings may include app data in a device or iCloud backup. Backups and files you export are separate from the app’s live storage and are managed through Apple or the destination you selected. Deleting local history does not recall a shared file.

You can edit preferences, delete individual or matching history records, manage tags and memory cards, and export history. Network access is necessary to fetch fresh quotes; offline calculation and available cached quotes do not require a new rate request.
