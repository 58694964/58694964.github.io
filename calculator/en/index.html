Support & Help

Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Basic and scientific calculation, memory and local history work offline. Currency conversion needs a previously downloaded quote; without one, the unavailable conversion is not presented as a valid amount.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

Fiat rates use Frankfurter, with ExchangeRate-API as a fallback. BTC reference prices use Yadio independently. These are reference rates, not bank or exchange execution prices.

How often do rates refresh?

On launch, the app checks whether at least 12 hours have passed since a successful fiat refresh. Entering BTC conversion checks its separate five-minute interval. Manual refresh bypasses these intervals, and a failed refresh retains the saved quotes.

Does changing decimal precision change the calculation?

No. Decimal and number-format settings change presentation, not the underlying calculation value. Copied or exported text reflects the displayed precision. Some scientific results and currency conversions are approximate.

Can I edit and reuse earlier work?

Move the cursor or select an operator in ordinary-number expressions. Currency input is edited at the end. History can restore a calculation; currency calculations then continue using the latest locally available rates, while the history card keeps its original snapshot.

Why does iOS ask before pasting?

Clipboard access happens when you choose Paste. iOS may ask for permission to paste from another app. The app does not monitor your clipboard in the background.

How can I remove my data?

Delete history records and memory cards in the app, or use Delete App in iOS to remove its local storage. Offloading preserves app data. Previously exported files and device backups must be managed separately.
